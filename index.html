🐹 About Ericka

The person behind the console

Ericka Florencia is a Vancouver-based sound designer working across film, television, and video-games. Formerly an instructor at Vancouver Film School, she currently engineers ADR at dbc sound inc. Her work is driven by a simple belief: sound should be deliberate, not sporadic — picked with intent so that nothing is missing, and nothing sticks out.

Workflow Enhancements

Open source · Contributor

A Pythonic wrapper for Audiokinetic's Wwise Authoring API (WAAPI), making it simple to script and automate Wwise projects from Python.
